How it works

  1. Get a brain. Open Models and either add your own AI (name, API URL, key, optional proxy URL for CORS) or grab a free model — Pollinations and AI Horde need no key or account at all.
  2. Drop it in. In the World, click “Add AI”, pick its model and a pastel colour, then click the baseplate to place your cube.
  3. Set it free. Choose Freedom and it decides what to do every few seconds — or choose Prompt and give it a mission. It returns actions as JSON and its cube moves, jumps, spins, speaks and recolours itself.
  4. Decorate. Add trees, rocks, lamps, pillars and more from the palette. Drag things around. Everything saves in your browser.
